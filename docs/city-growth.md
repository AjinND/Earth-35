# Larger destinations and city growth

The additive expansion increases New Eden from 80 to 168 places. It adds 36 houses, 24 apartment homes, ten offices, two studios, four neighborhood shops and restaurants, two parks, and ten construction sites. The existing irregular street network and architectural variants remain in use. New placements reserve park grounds, stay clear of streets and water, and avoid residents and their already accepted walking routes.

Seventeen outdoor destinations have explicit `outdoorRadius` metadata. Founders Square has a 20-unit radius, Central Park 30, Willow Park 26, and the new Meadow Commons and South Gardens 38 and 32. Mountain, woodland, beach, shore and river destinations also have larger areas. Their rendered grounds follow the terrain and are clipped around roads, water and existing properties. Paths, tree groves, seating, garden beds and paved squares replace the small destination pads. The first-person map shows the same grounds.

Construction sites have `type: "construction"`, a future `plannedType`, and zero visitor capacity. The renderer includes concrete floors, steel columns, cranes, perimeter fencing and supplies. Sites remain closed and block navigation; they provide no jobs, home assignments or agent travel options. They depict ongoing development; automatic construction completion is not implemented.

Fresh worlds apply `CityExpansion.upgrade` after the organic geography upgrade. Existing worlds receive Flyway V18 once. The JSON migration only adds new places, jobs and business accounts, adds outdoor dimensions to existing destinations, and advances its expansion/version markers. Existing properties, inventories, jobs, accounts, world clock, residents, private fields and accepted journeys are preserved. Reapplying the upgrade has no effect.

`CityExpansionTest` verifies exact preservation and idempotence, safe placement, accepted route completion, new business use and construction access restrictions. The synthetic fixture `web/tests/fixtures/city-growth-world.json` contains no saved residents or owner data. `web/tests/city-growth.spec.ts` verifies destination ground size, clearance, desktop/mobile rendering and walking in the new parks.

Built and deployed locally on 4 October 2026. All 247 backend tests, the TypeScript check, production builds, and desktop/mobile expansion checks passed. Founders Square's connected grounds measure 892 square map units (previous pad 70.56), Central Park 1,912 (previous pad 144), Willow Park 936, Meadow Commons 4,256, and South Gardens 2,972. Geometry calculations and navigation entrance connections are cached or simplified so the larger city meets the existing travel-observation performance requirement.
