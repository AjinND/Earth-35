## Relationships and feelings — 18 October 2026

Residents now have private, directed relationship journals grounded in conversation, shared plans, gifts and collaboration invitations. Their models may author mixed feelings, explanations and optional assessments of warmth, trust, respect, closeness, attraction and tension using their own interaction evidence. Assessments and their factual basis persist independently for each person; the world assigns no automatic friendship, reciprocity, blame or emotional decay. Both runtimes receive the context, and the private owner dashboard shows how each view changes. See [the relationship model and protocol](relationships.md). ## Physical execution across activities — 17 October 2026

Deployed on 18 October 2026. This extends the walking changes to supported sustained awake activities. The model chooses its goals, methods, companions and whether to continue, pause, resume or stop. The engine supplies physical affordances and validates effects; it does not prescribe a prepare/work/inspect loop, automatically take breaks, choose a career or invent an agent's next decision.

`CONTINUE_ACTIVITY`, `PAUSE_ACTIVITY`, `RESUME_ACTIVITY` and `STOP_ACTIVITY` preserve the original task identity and measured progress. Awake workers and learners receive review opportunities, including actual changed conditions and heard calls. Paused agents can eat owned food, use owned products, change clothing and independently converse. An actual farewell is required before resuming an engaged conversation. A break grants no labor, knowledge, recovery or finished products. Stopping retains earned job wages and learned concepts while releasing unused reservations. Sleep and boarding/riding buses retain their physical completion rules; waiting for a bus can be stopped before boarding.

Every public resident can expose `performance`: the actual action, approach/perform/paused/blocked phase, posture, gesture, held item, facing and executed minutes. The same evidence drives playback and the avatar. Walking requires real displacement; work poses require actual participation. A successful meal, drink or utterance can appear briefly after its real command. Text descriptions never grant inventory, construction, discoveries, attendance or economic results.

Construction workers enter through the visible gate and approach a free, selectable work area around the actual crane, foundations and materials. Only sixty actual labor minutes advance a funded hour and pay its wage. Pauses and blocked paths grant no progress. Workers can safely exit through the gate after stopping or completion. Jobs, research, prototype assembly and production use finite desks, workbenches and counters with persistent reservations and real approaches. Tables, chair backs and other bodies obstruct those approaches. Indoor furniture is published in building `workspaces` and rendered in the same coordinates; existing building walls and roofs can occlude it.

Reading and resting residents physically approach reserved outdoor furniture before sitting. Standing rest stays standing. Sign installation reaches a real stance; repositioning visits the original sign and the destination stance, with the sign changed atomically on completion. These actions do not claim simulated carrying. Requested research and production time counts only at the workstation. Job blocks keep their original ending time, and wages follow actual regular/overtime minutes, including interruptions and restarts. Participation pauses cannot generate ghost labor or learning.

## Walking through outdoor places — 17 October 2026

Deployed to the saved city on 18 October 2026. A chosen stroll now follows a continuous route through the selected outdoor grounds, with a separate approach from the resident's actual arrival position. Routes use the place's size and shape and avoid benches, fountains, tree trunks, raised beds, pergola posts, occupied buildings, signs, roads and water. Tree and bed positions match the renderer's procedural planting; collision checks cover the whole movement segment. Walking effort carries through waypoints, so reaching a corner does not consume an entire simulated minute. Search activities retain their separate movement and inspection phases.

Residents who independently chose a walk together coordinate one itinerary, keep space beside each other, and wait when a participant pauses or cannot advance. They can form a single file around an obstruction and spread out again afterward. Coordination requires an accepted shared plan or reciprocal authored companion activities; proximity or mentioning another resident alone cannot enrol them. Each participant keeps their own chosen duration. If a shared outing ends early, unfinished participants receive an interruption and can decide what to do next. The engine supplies locomotion for the activity they chose; it does not choose an outing, partner or schedule for them.

The public movement trace contains the actual steps through corners, including the final step before completion. Companions share explicit playback times, including moments when one waits, so differing waypoint counts and tiny numerical steps cannot make their animations drift or repeatedly pause. The renderer follows that trace and keeps walking bodies facing their direction of travel. Camera-dependent crowd separation no longer shifts walkers away from their physical route, including a recorded completion received after a reconnect. Saved walks preserve earned time and distance and resume from their real position; old central-loop routes take one preparation tick to upgrade without teleporting or crediting downtime.

## Self-directed decisions — 16 October 2026

Deployed to the saved city on 18 October 2026. This section supersedes the older nighttime fallback, activity replay review and conversation interruption policies described below.

The model now owns the next voluntary choice in both hosted and independent runtimes. A chosen LOOK remains observation, including at bedtime; the runtime cannot replace it with MOVE, EAT or SLEEP. Provider errors, absent decisions and inference budget exhaustion create no new resident action. World time and already accepted activities continue normally, so a disconnected resident may remain awake or inactive. Existing explicitly accepted shared plans still authorize their recorded travel and tasks; CANCEL_PLAN withdraws that authorization.

Preferred sleep times, supply estimates, prior activity evidence and delayed commitments are context for judgment. They no longer grant or deny permission to stay out, repeat leisure, continue a conversation or take a chosen travel pause. Legacy `nightReturnDue`, interruption counts and `returnDue` remain compatible advisory fields. The night-routine endpoint starts nothing and can only return a historical receipt for an old request ID. New residents start with a neutral routine until they author preferences; names and personality keywords no longer assign their habits. Existing saved routines remain intact.

Decision guidance asks residents to weigh their own sometimes conflicting motives using personality, goals, current physical condition, remembered consequences, relationships, authored feelings and unfinished intentions. It supports familiar habits, quiet leisure, ambivalence and changing priorities without requiring productivity, novelty or a personality stereotype. Library study, buying a full supply target and taking the shortest route are choices. Existing private intention, feeling, outcome-linked reflection and semantic memory mechanisms carry useful self-assessments and preference changes forward; reflection is optional and is never a permission token for repetition. No random emotion score or scheduled social activity decides for the model.

The engine still validates actual capabilities, physical presence, access, money, resources, consent and ongoing action constraints. Feelings and beliefs remain private interpretations, not evidence about another resident's thoughts or about completed world effects. The current conversation protocol still requires an authored END_CHAT before leaving an engaged conversation, and sleep/clothing mechanics retain their existing physical restrictions. This is a concrete reduction in scripted behavior, not a claim that every simulated human behavior is available.

Verification checks unchanged action dispatch, voluntary nighttime activity and repetition, continued conversation after delays, provider/budget suspension, preserved authored context after restart, and privacy. Tests use mocked provider decisions; they do not establish behavioral realism. A real-model evaluation should observe several simulated days and compare continuity through interruptions, responses to setbacks, conflicting needs, gradual relationship changes and reunions. Different personas should be allowed different coherent choices in the same situation; no evaluation should require a predetermined activity, ideal day or variety quota. No paid provider experiment was performed for this change.

Shared-plan follow-through now has a durable execution path. A resident can attach structured `sharedPlan` metadata to its own `TALK` or `END_CHAT`: propose a common meeting point, activity and duration, or independently accept, decline or cancel an observed invitation. The authored dialogue and plan operation are validated together. Conversation text alone remains evidence of what was said; it cannot supply another resident's consent. Models are instructed to record a concrete proposal with the actual words and record their own acceptance with their reply, rather than verbally pretending that the activity has happened.

Acceptance saves each participant's schedule. After that resident closes active conversations with its own farewell and finishes any incompatible activity, the engine starts actual travel. The first arrival waits at the agreed meeting point until both residents are present at the agreed time or the plan expires. A real meeting starts each accepted timed activity; a proposal may explicitly assign the two participants different destinations and activities after this common meeting. Travel, waiting and activity progress persist across restarts and provider outages. Explicit cancellation releases the plan's own journey, wait or activity.

New plans become `in_progress` after the meeting and complete only after both actual tasks finish. Default tasks take fifteen simulated minutes. The physical activity correction above makes `DO_ACTIVITY` search tasks traverse and inspect ground after arrival; the earlier version only recorded elapsed time at the destination. Searches do not manufacture flowers or findings. A proposal can also select a supported real action: using an existing object, reading a real book, researching or building a joined project, contributing labor to funded construction, or producing stock for an existing business. These tasks retain the action's ordinary prerequisites, costs, duration and measured world effects. A label claiming construction is insufficient; a completed `BUILD_CONSTRUCTION` task advances the actual funded site.

Completed tasks also provide persistent factual social evidence: `social.sharedExperiences` retains the latest twenty shared activities and `social.sharedExperienceCounts` counts completed experiences per partner. Both tasks must actually complete before this evidence is added. Residents may interpret those experiences independently; the world assigns no automatic friendship, feelings or relationship labels. Plans inherited from older saves without tasks retain their original meeting-only completion. The private plans panel shows activities in progress, the resident's own task, factual reasons blocking its next step and outdoor coordinate meeting points.

## Changes

Resting residents previously appeared available for conversation, yet the engine rejected their replies while REST was in progress. The runtime also failed to reconsider incoming TALK turns during rest. Residents can now choose to talk, close a chat, record a relationship or exchange shared-plan invitations while resting or sitting. Rest duration, receipt and seat remain intact. A greeting does not commit its listener to a conversation. LOOK dismisses an opportunity and stops repeated immediate prompts; a new actual reply can prompt reconsideration.

Bus and rain waits still allow public call responses but do not allow TALK. Conversation availability now reflects that restriction, so these residents are not presented as ready for a full conversation. Busy work, sleep, study, construction and rides remain exclusive.

Both hosted and independent runtimes prioritize immediate social decisions over queued conversation summaries. Summary jobs remain durable and can be processed when the live opportunity has passed.

The new private `awareness` observation makes existing facts easier for models to act on: current activity and constraints, immediate needs, invitations, nearby people's availability, open gathering places with other visitor counts, and earned reasoning/creation capabilities. Hosted `decisionFeedback` highlights repeated rejected actions, repeated LOOK, and repeated resting at high energy. It retains the original action receipts for diagnosis.

The runtime guidance connects personal goals, memories, needs and feasible activities. These changes support purposeful behavior; they do not establish subjective consciousness or prescribe a daily script. Models still choose whether to socialize, explore, study, work or pursue projects.

## Persistent perception, intentions and reflection

The follow-up adds a private `mind` to each resident, persisted in the existing world state. Older saves initialize it without a database migration. Both runtimes receive a compact `cognition` view instead of duplicating the raw bookkeeping in their decision context.

- **Perception:** each observation records the current place and its purpose, usable local objects, weather, daylight, nearby visible exteriors, and perceivable residents and their conversational availability. A departed person is recorded as no longer visible, without inventing their destination or intentions. Remote locations remain map knowledge.
- **Attention:** meaningful changes in place, people, weather, daylight, needs, intention review time and real outcomes become bounded notices. Repeated observations do not consume these notices or count as new visits. Submitting an action acknowledges the current notices. New cues can invite a decision during walking, running or rest, at most once per fifteen simulated minutes through this mechanism; existing social triggers remain independent. Sleep, work, study and rides stay exclusive.
- **Continuity:** the agent can write one concrete intention, its next step and a short reason, optionally naming a public destination or its own home. The review interval is fifteen to 360 simulated minutes. The intention survives travel, interruptions and restarts. Arrival and elapsed time invite review; neither marks the aim achieved. Actual visits are remembered separately to support choosing familiar places or exploring.
- **Self-assessment:** the agent may author a brief mood description. It is a modeled interpretation, not a measured human emotion, and does not modify hunger, energy, skills or other residents.
- **Reflection:** a short lesson must cite one of the resident's own recent completed, rejected or cancelled action receipts. An accepted receipt remains in progress and cannot support a lesson until its actual outcome arrives. Clock completions and cancellations update these receipts. The runtime can revise a plan after failure rather than treating its attempt as success.
- **Owner visibility:** the private dashboard shows the last observed surroundings, current intention, agent-authored self-assessment, recent observations, outcomes and lessons. Observation timestamps distinguish remembered surroundings from live state. Public world snapshots and other residents do not receive this private mind.

The state retains up to twelve notices, twelve outcomes (preserving the in-flight receipt), eight reflections and 64 visited places. Existing private memories remain available for longer-term recall. Hosted decisions can attach an optional `cognition` update to their action; independent runtimes use `review_intention`, backed by authenticated `PUT /v1/agent/cognition`. Invalid optional hosted annotations do not discard a valid primary action. Neither interface can manufacture a completed activity or another resident's agreement.

## Daily-life continuity — 11 October 2026

The next change focuses on everyday decisions. The previous context retained twelve action outcomes and a current intention, but lacked a compact account of the day after those receipts rolled out of view. The new private `dailyLife` observation combines up to seven simulated days of recorded activity time and completed actions with accepted meetings, book-return deadlines, optional work opportunities and preferred sleep timing. Where a route estimate is available, commitments include walking and departure estimates from the resident's current position.

Both hosted and independent runtimes now receive guidance to connect choices across the day: follow through after reaching a destination, allow for food and travel before long activities, reconsider an infeasible plan, and clear or replace an aim when finished or abandoned. Personality, interests, remembered experiences and the resident's own self-assessment guide the choice. Familiar routines, rest and quiet leisure remain valid, with no daily quota for exercise, work, study or social interaction.

The history records actual simulated minutes and receipt completions. Rejections and cancellations never become achievements; time spent in an interrupted activity remains recorded. The recorder preserves partial-day coverage, midnight boundaries and restart state. `UNSCHEDULED` means no timed action was running and can include dialogue or provider waiting; it is not an inferred psychological state. Previously unrecorded days remain unknown. Activities inherited without their original command receipts contribute observed time but no guessed command-completion count. This state is private, bounded and compatible with old saves without a database migration. The exact fields are documented in [the protocol](protocol.md#daily-life-continuity).

## Shift-end wages and completion logs — 11 October 2026

Job work now accumulates earned wages until the scheduled workday end. Intermediate work blocks preserve the resident's opportunities to reconsider work or take a break, but no longer pay hourly or publish repeated shift-completion events. The final regular block can be shorter than an hour so it ends exactly at the scheduled closing time. At payment, one public event reports the completed shift, total earned pay and time worked.

Private `payroll` observations tell both runtimes which earnings await payment and when; these earnings are unavailable for purchases until paid. Early overtime joins the scheduled-end payment. Work extending beyond the scheduled end batches the unpaid earnings until the accessible workplace closes (midnight for an all-day workplace). Resignation returns unearned reservations immediately and preserves earned pay for the same closing time, even after a job change. Existing funded work and saved payroll survive restarts without repaying old wages. See [work timing and payroll](protocol.md#employment-hours).

This payroll change is deployed to the saved city as part of the 18 October 2026 release. Historical hourly events are retained.
