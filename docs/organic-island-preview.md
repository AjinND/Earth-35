# Organic island, geography v2

Built and deployed locally on 4 October 2026. Fresh worlds use geography version 2, and Flyway V17 upgrades existing worlds once. The saved island was backed up before migration; its four residents and 76 existing places were preserved, and four wilderness destinations were added.

The layout includes irregular neighborhood clusters without individual grid plots, fourteen winding streets with incomplete blocks and T junctions, five house designs and five apartment designs, a descending river from the northern ridge to the sea, and two footbridges. Woodland continues through undeveloped parts of the settlement. The northern scenic destinations sit on the island terrain.

Residents can use `MOVE` or `RUN` with `target: "point:x,z"` to select any dry-land coordinate. Examples: `point:-225,-450` (northern ridge), `point:-595,-100` (western woods), and `point:-150,350` (south shore). The observation's `exploration` describes the coastline and this syntax. Routes avoid building interiors, ocean and the river, using footbridges to cross water. `REST` is available after arrival on dry landscape coordinates. Cameras and avatars follow terrain height, and the first-person map covers the whole island.

`OrganicCity.upgrade` changes only layout fields in saved JSON, retaining unknown and private fields. Existing building identities, capacities, business inventories, jobs, ownership, balances, routines and relationships survive. Accepted walks are recalculated while retaining their receipt IDs; bus passengers retain their itinerary and paid fare state while remaining arrival times follow the curved-road forecast. Physical lane width and vehicle clearance are preserved through curves, and junction reservations allow buses to turn without blocking one another. `OrganicCity.preview(saved)` remains available for detached previews.

The reproducible browser snapshot is `web/tests/fixtures/organic-world.json`. Backend coverage is in `OrganicIslandTest` and `OrganicMigrationTest`, and browser and geometry coverage is in `web/tests/organic-island.spec.ts`. The preview suite verifies desktop and mobile rendering and first-person mountain and beach travel. `web/tests/organic-live.spec.ts` checks the actual deployed API and explores the mountain, western woods, southern beach and eastern shore. These checks do not create accounts or residents.

Backend coverage includes a full simulated day of curved-road vehicle collision checks, saved paid/waiting bus journeys, private-field preservation, idempotent migration and navigation across the island. The live browser test reached and walked on the mountain, western woodland, southern beach and eastern shore without page errors. API logs showed no errors.
